الأطفال

«وضع الأطفال» لمن هم أقل من 16 سنة بيقلل الخيارات ويقفل الإعدادات برقم سري يعرفه ولي الأمر. ده قفل بسيط على الجهاز مش حماية قوية. لا نطلب من الطفل اسمًا أو إيميلًا أو صورة.

حذف البيانات

زر «إعادة ضبط التطبيق» في الإعدادات بيمسح كل شيء محفوظ على الجهاز. حذف أي بيانات على السيرفر (بعد تفعيل Supabase) لسه مش متاح من داخل التطبيق: تواصل معنا وهنمسحها.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 (draft)

What stays on your device

Your stories, reading progress, favorites, settings, saved voice recordings and cached audio live in your browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) and are not sent anywhere. The parent PIN for kids mode is stored on the device as a salted hash, never sent to the server.

What is sent to our server

  • Natural voice: the text chunk to be read, sent to the configured text-to-speech provider.
  • Translation and diacritics: the story text, sent to the configured AI provider.
  • URL import: the link you enter. The server fetches the public page and extracts text.
  • Activation code: sent once to be checked. Only a hash of the code is stored.
  • Anonymous session and rate limits: a random session id (no name or email). Your IP address is turned into a keyed hash to limit abuse and is not stored as is.

PDFs and scanned pages (OCR) are processed on your device and are not uploaded.

What we do not do

No ads, no third-party trackers, no selling of data. Fonts and OCR files are served from this site, not third-party servers.

Children

Kids mode (under 16) reduces options and locks settings behind a parent PIN. This is a simple on-device lock, not strong security. We do not ask a child for a name, email or photo.

Deleting data

"Reset app" in Settings erases everything stored on the device. Deleting server-side data (once Supabase is enabled) is not yet available in the app. Contact us and we will remove it.
